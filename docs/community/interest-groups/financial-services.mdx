## Group Type

**Interest Group**

## Mission Statement

The Financial Services Interest Group (FSIG) brings together participants from regulated financial institutions to identify shared MCP
adoption and interoperability problems, exchange implementation experience, and connect those needs to existing protocol capabilities and
community work. Its default approach is to first identify how existing MCP primitives and publicly shareable industry patterns address a
problem, then bring any unresolved protocol gap to the appropriate Working Group (WG). Where no WG covers a widely acknowledged problem with
a clear potential deliverable, FSIG helps gather cross-community interest and contributors to propose a WG through [MCP group governance](/community/working-interest-groups#forming-a-group).

FSIG may discuss and recommend potential solutions, and collaborate with relevant Working Groups to prototype approaches, including
exploratory finance-specific extensions.

## Scope

### In Scope

- **Auditability and operational control**. Identify what a financial institution needs to know about an MCP tool call to review an action or investigate an incident.
- **Data lineage and citation**. Identify how clients can trace financial data returned through MCP to its source and preserve citations when presenting it.
- **Data handling**. Examine what information a client needs when a tool result contains data subject to usage or redistribution restrictions.
- **Problem discovery and shared implementation experience**: collecting cross-institution use cases, documenting common constraints, and
  sharing public examples of how existing MCP primitives and complementary implementation patterns can be combined within the current spec
- **Solution exploration**: discussing and, where useful, prototyping possible approaches or experimental extensions to test feasibility
  and clarify requirements, without treating FSIG as the default owner of standards-track deliverables
- **Interoperability across institutions**: identifying reusable patterns and gaps while avoiding institution-specific prescriptions or
  duplicative protocol development
- **WG collaboration and referrals**: connecting unresolved problems, evidence and interested participants to existing WGs responsible for
  the relevant technical scope
- **New WG incubation**: when a broadly relevant gap has no existing WG, gathering cross-IG and wider community interest, potential
  contributors and a clear problem statement to support a proposed WG under the [group formation
  rules](/community/working-interest-groups#forming-a-group)
- **SEP input**: contributing problem statements, use cases, implementation experience and draft proposals in collaboration with relevant
  WGs

### Out of Scope

- **Independent ownership of standards-track protocol changes**: FSIG does not independently sponsor SEPs or substitute for a WG's
  delegated technical decision-making and delivery role; exploratory solutions and extensions may inform WG work
- **Competitively sensitive or non-public business information**: pricing, costs, margins, customer lists, market segmentation, and
  competitive strategy, per the [MCP Antitrust Policy](/community/antitrust)
- **General security threat modeling**: MCP-wide attack-surface analysis belongs to the [Security IG](/community/interest-groups/security).
  This group provides financial-sector requirements as input and consumes its threat models
- **Authorization protocol mechanics**: OAuth flows, scopes, client registration, and token handling belong to the [Authorization
  IG](/community/interest-groups/auth)
- **Product-specific compliance guides**: step-by-step configuration for an individual host application, cloud platform, or institution is
  documentation for that product rather than protocol work
- **Legal, regulatory, and commercial advice or coordination**: legal interpretation, regulatory policy positions, procurement, commercial
  terms, and engagement with regulators or industry bodies on their behalf

### Related Groups

- FSIG shares financial-sector incident review use cases with the [Security IG](/community/interest-groups/security) and draws on its threat models.
- **[Authorization IG](/community/interest-groups/auth)**: identity and access control for sensitive financial data sit at the boundary
  between the two groups
- **[Tool Annotations IG](/community/interest-groups/tool-annotations)**: trust and sensitivity annotations are directly relevant to
  financial tool exposure; the FSIG provides requirements as input
- FSIG brings use cases involving the observation of tool calls to the [Interceptors WG](/community/working-groups/interceptors).
- **[Registry WG](/community/working-groups/registry)**: server provenance and publishing metadata intersect with financial supply-chain
  and admission concerns

## Operations

FSIG meets **monthly, for up to 60 minutes** initially, as an open industry forum for sharing common problems, exchanging experience with
existing MCP capabilities, and helping participants find relevant Working Groups and opportunities to contribute. Facilitators may revisit
the cadence if recurring topics, sustained interest, or the volume of proposed agenda items warrants more frequent meetings.

Meetings use a lightweight, community-driven agenda, not fixed time allocations. Recurring prompts may include introductions and ways to get
involved, shared problems, existing approaches using MCP primitives, relevant WG/IG work, and follow-ups. Facilitators invite agenda
suggestions in a thread in `#financial-services-ig` ahead of each meeting; anyone can reply with topics or questions. The discussion remains
free-flowing, with Facilitators helping route detailed technical work to WGs.

The agenda or a link to it is posted in the GitHub Discussions Meeting Notes category before the meeting, and notes are published to that
discussion within 48 hours, as required by [MCP meeting governance](/community/working-interest-groups#meetings-and-channels). Calendar
entries are published at least seven days ahead, meetings are open to all, and the Code of Conduct and Antitrust Policy apply. This
Discord-first agenda workflow follows the approaches used by the [Security IG](/community/interest-groups/security) and [Authorization IG](/community/interest-groups/auth).

Discord: [#financial-services-ig](https://discord.gg/modelcontextprotocol)

## Discussion Topics

The following are illustrative examples of topics relevant to MCP adoption in financial services, not predefined deliverables. This list is
not exhaustive and will evolve based on community interest.

| Topic                                    | Illustrative discussion question                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Auditability and operational control** | If a connection drops after an order is submitted, what can the client establish about the outcome?                          |
| **Data lineage and citation**            | Can a reader trace a reported figure to the filing and reporting period it came from?                                        |
| **Data handling**                        | When a result combines licensed market data with a public filing, can the client tell which data the restriction applies to? |
